## ✨ Features & Highlights

### 🎧 Audiophile Audio Engine & Studio DSP
* **Bit-Perfect Lossless FLAC:** Native high-resolution audio streaming up to 24-bit / 96kHz.
* **DSEE Extreme™ AI Overtone Synthesizer:** Real-time AI harmonic reconstruction that restores lost high-frequency details (up to 24+ kHz) on compressed audio, delivering silky smooth transients.
* **Studio-Grade 64-Band FFT:** Phase-accurate hardware-accelerated real-time spectrum analysis and visualizer embedded into the player.
* **DJ Stem Deck & Vocal Remover:** Real-time vocal attenuation for karaoke or multi-channel stem mixing directly on your device.

### 🔒 Decentralized, Private & Account-Free
* **Zero Accounts / Zero Telemetry:** No user registration, no profiling, no analytics, no ads.
* **Pulse Connect (Tor P2P Mesh):** Connect directly with your friends or between your own devices. Stream audio synchronously in real-time, or use your PC as a wireless Hi-Fi speaker.
* **Decentralized Resilience:** Fully autonomous architecture that requires no central account servers.

### 📥 One-Click Playlist Import
* **Spotify & Deezer Import:** Seamlessly import any public playlist via link — no Spotify credentials or premium accounts required.
* **Rich Metadata Integration:** Automatic high-resolution artwork fetching, artist biographies, complete discographies, and synchronized time-coded lyrics (LRC).
